PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Avant Garde Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Habit Tracker app ("the App").

1. DATA COLLECTION

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with us or with third parties.

The App does not operate a remote server or backend and does not send user data to any remote endpoint.

The App does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling services.

2. DATA STORED ON THE DEVICE

Information entered into the App, including habits, habit completion records, preferences, and related app data, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is processed solely on the device for the purpose of providing the App's functionality.

Avant Garde Labs does not have access to this locally stored data.

3. NETWORK ACCESS

The App does not require a remote network service to operate. User data is not uploaded to Avant Garde Labs or any third-party server.

4. BACKUP AND RESTORE

The App may provide backup and restore functionality. Any backup file created by the App is controlled by the user and may be stored or transferred using the device's operating system or services selected by the user.

Avant Garde Labs does not receive or control such backups.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services.

The operating system and app distribution platform may independently collect information according to their own privacy policies. Such collection is outside the control of Avant Garde Labs.

6. DATA DELETION

Because App data is stored locally, the user can generally delete it using the App's available deletion functionality, by clearing the App's data through the operating system, or by uninstalling the App.

Avant Garde Labs does not retain a remote copy of the user's App data.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since the App does not transmit user data to us, there is no user data collection by Avant Garde Labs through the App.

8. SECURITY

User data remains on the user's device. The security of locally stored data also depends on the security controls provided by the device's operating system and the user's device configuration.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date.
